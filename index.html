About Tonalette

Music fans first, AI second

Tonalette means a palette of tones. AI is one of the instruments we play. The taste, the ideas and the final call stay with people who love music.

1

We obsess

We listen to everything, take songs apart and study why a chorus lands or a mix breathes. That homework goes into every track.

2

We make

We write the lyrics, set the style and run take after take with AI tools until the song sounds the way we heard it in our heads.

3

We review

A person listens to every song from start to finish: lyrics, vocals, mix, odd artefacts. If it is not right, it does not get released.

Every AI song we release is checked by a human.No exceptions, and every release is clearly labelled as AI-generated.
